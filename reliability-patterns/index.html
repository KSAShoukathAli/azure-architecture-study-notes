Reliability & Idempotency Patterns

design for ambiguity, duplicates, retries, and recovery
The mental model: distributed systems do not fail cleanly. A dependency may finish the work while the acknowledgement is lost. A consumer may commit a side effect and crash before settling the message. Reliability comes from making retries bounded, duplicate execution harmless, and recovery explicit.
REFERENCE

Architect Quick Reference

Retries, retry budgets, circuit breakers, idempotent consumers, Service Bus duplicate detection, DLQs, transactions, outbox/inbox, saga compensation, replay, and Azure-specific failure behavior.

 FEYNMAN

Feynman Way of Understanding

Start with the uncomfortable question: "The call timed out - did the other side already do the work?" Derive the patterns from that ambiguity.
one sentence

Reliable integration assumes duplicates can happen, then makes them harmless.
