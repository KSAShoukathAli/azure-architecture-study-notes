FEYNMAN

End-to-End Observability - Feynman Way

Start with one slow business transaction and derive why metrics, logs, traces, business IDs, queue timing, and alerts are all different pieces of the same reconstruction problem.

core model

Metrics tell you that something is wrong. Traces tell you where time and failures moved. Logs explain local detail. Business correlation tells you which real transaction the evidence belongs to.

do not confuse

Logging everything is not observability. A terabyte of unrelated log lines can still leave you unable to answer: which request failed, where did the time go, what message carried it, and what business record was affected?
